# 0.1.6 — 2026-07-26

## Changed

- **`content[]` numeric display rounds away floating-point noise** — a new `displayNumber()` helper (`src/mcp-server/tools/shared/format-helpers.ts`) rounds measurement values and summary stats in `openaq_get_measurements` and `openaq_get_readings`'s rendered text (4 decimal places at or above 1, 4 significant digits below 1). `structuredContent` and canvas-staged values are unchanged and exact. ([#10](https://github.com/cyanheads/openaq-mcp-server/issues/10))

## Fixed

- **`OpenAqMeasurement.value` widens to `number | null`** through `SeriesRow`, the `openaq_get_measurements` output schema, and `format()` — a gap bucket (a period the sensor reported nothing into) previously failed output validation and dropped the entire series; it now renders as "no data" with no unit. `OpenAqSummary`'s `avg`/`max`/`median`/`min`/`q02`/`q25`/`q75`/`q98` are also nullable, matching the already-nullable `sd`. ([#11](https://github.com/cyanheads/openaq-mcp-server/issues/11))
- **`openaq_get_measurements` normalizes date and date-time range bounds to full UTC before comparing or forwarding** — a date-only lower bound now opens at `00:00:00Z` and an upper bound closes at `23:59:59Z`, fixing both a same-day date-only range (previously rejected as zero-width) and a mixed date/datetime range (previously misclassified locally or forwarded to OpenAQ in a shape that 500s). ([#6](https://github.com/cyanheads/openaq-mcp-server/issues/6))
- **A failed page mid-pull no longer discards the series** — `openaq_get_measurements`'s internal pager keeps the rows already fetched and returns them with a `notice` naming the failed page, instead of throwing away a partial pull. The `MAX_ROWS` (5000) cap now discloses itself in the `notice` rather than only setting `truncated: true`; the `canvasId`/`truncated`/`tableName` output descriptions and the README's DataCanvas section no longer claim the canvas holds "the full series". `OpenAqService.throwForStatus()` classifies a 408 as `Timeout` with `retryable: false`, so `withRetry` fails fast instead of burning three attempts on a page offset that 408s on every replay. ([#12](https://github.com/cyanheads/openaq-mcp-server/issues/12))
- **A canvas that's configured but fails to start now degrades instead of failing the call** — `openaq_get_measurements` catches a throw from `canvas.acquire()`/`registerTable()` and returns the truncated preview with a `notice` naming the staging failure, preserving the rows already fetched. A caller-supplied `canvas_id` that can't be resolved still throws `canvas_not_found`. ([#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/openaq-mcp-server/issues/19))
- **`openaq_get_measurements`'s text meta line now matches what it renders** — reads "20 of 100 preview rows shown" when rows are held back, instead of claiming all 100 were shown, with a footer pointing at `structuredContent.series` for the rest. ([#15](https://github.com/cyanheads/openaq-mcp-server/issues/15))
